During the lot of work through the summer of 2026 I managed to take a quick trip to Spain to witness the solar eclipse (which is an amazing
astronomical event, not hapenning every friday). With the added benefit of being relatively close and easy to travel to I could pack my gear with me
and test a few things.

I tried capturing the eclipse on a RPi HQ camera, with a Samyang 135mm EF lens, mounted onto a Pinefeat Canon EF adapter (this gives
about 5.5x crop ratio and rougly 750mm lens equivalent). All was mouned atop an iOptron SGPro tracker, to stabilize the capture during the critical 
90 seconds (even though the wind was interfering with that a bit).

I managed to capture some images, though they proved to have a relatively low dynamic range to show the Earthshine I am happy that I at least got something
that looks interesting. And most that that got to experience the whole event first hand, with my friends from the Astronomy expedition, with which we
met at large numbers in Madrid.
